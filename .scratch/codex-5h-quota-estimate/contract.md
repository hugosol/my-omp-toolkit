## Seam decisions

| Seam | Exposes | Hides | Alternatives considered | Reversibility |
|------|---------|-------|------------------------|---------------|
| Mounted extension surface — the extension mounted against a fake ExtensionAPI, with lifecycle events, provider responses, commands, a substituted archive home, and a controlled clock; observed through the rendered widget factory, request and timer counts, and the document bytes it writes. | Appended segment presence and absence, `estimating…`, label order, semantic colour, width degradation, request and timer censuses, command behaviour, incompatible-version message. | Estimator arithmetic and pairing rules, rollover precedence, throttle bookkeeping, document layout, lock usage, render composition, which window values the display merge kept. | (a) One seam for everything, including the document promises — rejected: loses diagnosis locality, forces hand-choreographed threshold cases, makes schema assertions indirect. (b) Promoting the estimator's internal interface to a contract seam — rejected: an internal seam is the agent's business. (c) A third seam for the usage-provider delivery — rejected: its promises are already carried here and the module-loader stand-in is the existing adapter. | High — internals rewrite freely; only the appended text's observable facts are pinned. |
| Estimate state document surface — the persisted document observed under a substituted archive home, published through the existing lock adapter. | Publish-then-load round trip and the readable document shape with a nullable ratio, baseline-first publication, first-run behaviour on absent, legacy or corrupt documents, immediate baseline publication versus throttled ratio cadence, retry after a failed write, concurrent publish without torn reads, newer-baseline merge that never downgrades a known ratio. | Storage location, temp-file and rename retry, lock acquisition and backoff, caching, serialization details, everything beyond the document fields. | (a) Fold the document promises into the mounted surface — rejected: schema, cadence and atomicity are promises about the document itself, and the repository already observes its daily archive this way. (b) Expose a store interface with an injected lock — rejected: the lock is an internal detail and a test-only hook in the interface is indirection. | Medium — the readable shape and no-version decision are settled; assertions stay at shape level rather than literal JSON. |

Dependency stand-ins (all existing, reused; none adds a contract seam):

- The OMP usage provider and header parser are a true external dependency; paired readings arrive through the existing fake module-loader stand-in.
- The estimate state document is local-substitutable: production uses the real archive home and the native cross-process lock; tests substitute a temporary archive home and the in-process lock chain.
- The clock is an in-process input, controlled through the existing `now` parameter or `Date.now` pattern; no adapter is needed.
- The estimator and the weekly render composition remain internal seams of the extension: their interfaces are free behind the two surfaces and are not part of this contract.

## Not yet specified

- How the estimator and the readable document would extend to additional quota windows or additional limit kinds (Spark meters, daily, monthly, annual) if the area grows; the current shape is not designed for multiple window pairs.
- Whether any future feature consumes the estimate state document as an input.
- The internal boundary between estimation and render composition, and whether focused tests exercise an internal estimator seam directly.
- Multi-account attribution, which the spec places out of scope, remains unspecified here as well.
